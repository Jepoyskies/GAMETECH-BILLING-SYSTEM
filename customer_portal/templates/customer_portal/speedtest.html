How to Get the Most Accurate Speed Test
  • Use 5GHz Wi-Fi or LAN Cable:
    Standard 2.4GHz Wi-Fi has physical limits around 40-50 Mbps. Connect to your router's 5G network or use a LAN cable for full fiber speed.
  • Pause Heavy Downloads:
    Ensure no other devices in your home are downloading games, 4K streaming, or syncing cloud backups during the test.
  • Stay Near the Modem:
    Concrete walls and metal appliances can weaken Wi-Fi signals. Test in the same room as your router.
