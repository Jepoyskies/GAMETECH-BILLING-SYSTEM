{% comment %} File a REPAIR from the subscriber's own record. WHY THIS EXISTS --------------- The customer page could suspend, kick, edit a balance, edit an expiry, mark someone installed and reset a portal password -- but there was no way to send a customer to dispatch. Staff had to leave the record, open Dispatch and re-type the same name, number and address by hand, so repairs were raised inconsistently or not at all. That is the step the whole business runs on. It files a REPAIR ticket, which is already fully wired downstream: the dispatch queue, analytics, reports, technician mobile and QA all understand ticket_type='REPAIR'. Assigning a technician stays a separate, deliberate action in the dispatch queue. Filing a ticket is our own business record and touches no router. {% endcomment %}
