{% comment %} Unified Customer Status Cell ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ONE badge, driven by customer.lifecycle (billing/customer_state.py), which combines the billing axis and the hardware axis into a single state staff can act on. Billing, hardware and installation stay three separate domains internally (AGENTS.md rule 35) -- they are just presented as one verdict so the row is readable at a glance. The label is the whole point. "Active" alone was ambiguous: it could mean paid, online, or merely flagged active in a legacy export. Now the label always states BOTH facts, e.g. "Connected, Unpaid" or "Paid, Offline". The two dots stay separate on purpose: router reachability and the customer's own session are different faults with different fixes. "Unknown" is rendered as a neutral dot, never as a red "offline" dot -- we must not accuse a customer of an outage we cannot actually see.
