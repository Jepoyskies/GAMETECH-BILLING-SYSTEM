Zero-Downtime Router Transfer & Hardware Replacement Architecture

Solved the critical field maintenance challenge where migrating subscribers between Mikrotik routers for scheduled router swaps caused immediate subscriber disconnection before the hardware was physically replaced:

  • The Problem: When updating a customer's router assignment or performing a Sync Manager move, Django's post-save signal executed "Orphan Cleanup" on the old router using delete_pppoe_user(). By default, secret deletion also forcefully kicked the subscriber's active session, abruptly severing their internet connection while the old router was still in service.
  • Decoupled Session Lifecycles: Upgraded network_manager/services/users.py to accept a kick_active=True/False parameter. In billing/signals.py, orphan cleanup now runs with kick_active=False.
  • Zero Service Interruption: The old PPPoE secret is removed (preventing future logins), but the established active PPPoE interface session remains fully connected and operational on Router A until field technicians physically swap the hardware, at which point the ONT authenticates smoothly onto Router B. Documented in gametech_error_runbook.md under ERR-018.
