Tech Troubleshooting Guide
  • Connect via LAN / 5GHz Wi-Fi:
    2.4GHz Wi-Fi maxes out around 40-60 Mbps due to local 2.4GHz band congestion. Always test on 5GHz or Cat6 Ethernet.
  • Check Optical Power (dBm):
    Target optical reception power is -15 dBm to -24 dBm. Levels worse than -27 dBm cause frame drops and packet jitter.
  • MikroTik Bandwidth Test:
    Use Winbox Terminal: /tool bandwidth-test address=202.61.110.153 protocol=tcp for raw router-to-router throughput.
